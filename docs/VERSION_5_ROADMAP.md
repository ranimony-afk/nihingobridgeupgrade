# Version 5 Enterprise Strategic Roadmap

**Long-Term Platform Horizon (Phases 11 – 15)**

---

## 1. Roadmap Initiatives

### Phase 11 — AI Tutor Microservice Integration
- Conversational Japanese AI Assistant and Engineering Design Coach.
- Ingestion of structured course outlines via `/api/v1/courses/:slug` and `/api/v1/nihongo/items`.
- Real-time grammar correction and Japanese pronunciation assessment.

### Phase 12 — Marketplace & Global Monetization Engine
- Stripe & Apple/Google In-App Purchases (IAP) integration.
- Certified career programs and premium JLPT N2/N1 mock test packages.

### Phase 13 — Enterprise Community & Peer Learning
- Discussion forums, code review threads, and Japanese conversation partner matching.

### Phase 14 — Native Mobile Applications (Flutter / Swift / Kotlin)
- Cross-platform Flutter client consuming `/api/v1/mobile/*` with local SQLite offline caching.

### Phase 15 — Global Multilingual Expansion
- Ingesting translation memories for **Hindi (`hi`)**, **German (`de`)**, **French (`fr`)**, and **Korean (`ko`)**.
