# Headless CMS Administration Setup & Manual

**Document Version:** 4.0.0  
**Access Path:** `/admin`  

---

## 1. Accessing the Workspace

To enter the administration panel:
1. Ensure your local or cloud database is migrated and seeded.
2. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:3000/admin`.
3. If social login is enabled, authenticate using Google OAuth, or use credentials provider bypasses in local development.

---

## 2. Navigating the 11-Manager Sidebar

Our transformed administration console provides unified control across 11 managers:

- **📊 Dashboard Analytics**: View real-time KPIs, active student streaks, and Monthly XP charts.
- **📝 Headless CMS Content**: Toggle page selector tabs (`home`, `about`, `privacy_policy`, etc.) to edit section cards, modify position placements, duplicate drafts, and restore snapshots.
- **🌐 Multilingual Engine**: Perform split-screen side-by-side English &rarr; target language translations. Read Missing Translation logs to find gaps.
- **📂 Digital Media DAM**: Register new assets and view accessibility alt tags, WebP/AVIF srcset maps, and duplicates checks.
- **🎓 LMS Course Manager**: Create, list, and verify curriculum levels and tracks.
- **📰 Blog & Daily News**: Edit daily NHK Easy and advanced Todaii current articles.
- **📖 Vocabulary & Kanji**: Browse 64 characters and master readings.
- **⚙️ Site & SEO Settings**: Modify navigation links and meta tags.
- **📜 Audit Logging Trails**: Scroll through a detailed administrative trail.
